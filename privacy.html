Website Information

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Information you provide

When you contact TEKVANZA through the website, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in, and the contents of your message. Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, national ID information, or other sensitive information through the contact form.

How information is processed

The website contact form is configured to submit messages through Formspree, a third-party form processing provider. Information submitted through that form is processed by Formspree and made available to the TEKVANZA account configured for the form. Formspree may process information under its own terms and privacy policy. For job applications, applicants may email their CV at any time, including when no vacancy is listed. The applicant's email provider will process that message and any attached CV.

Purpose and retention

Information is used to respond to enquiries, assess service requirements, and handle recruitment enquiries. TEKVANZA should retain submitted information only for as long as needed for these purposes and applicable legal or business requirements. Please contact us if you want to ask about a message you submitted.

Analytics and cookies

Google Analytics tracking has been disabled in this release while the analytics property ownership and an appropriate privacy/consent setup are being confirmed. Essential browser storage may be used by site features such as remembering your selected language.

Contact

For privacy questions about this website, contact TEKVANZA at info@tekvanza.com.
