TEKVANZA — Careers page setup (current implementation)
======================================================

The Careers page accepts open applications year-round, including when no
vacancy is advertised.

How applicants apply:
1. Open careers.html.
2. Select "Email Your CV".
3. Their email application opens addressed to info@tekvanza.com.
4. They must attach their CV manually before sending.

Important:
- There is no careers web form or automated CV upload.
- No Formspree Careers endpoint is required.
- The applicant's email client must be configured to send email.
- The email address and open-application process were confirmed by the owner.

Before launch:
- Test the mailto link on desktop and mobile.
- Confirm info@tekvanza.com can receive attachments.
- Review the Privacy Policy against actual CV access and retention practices.
